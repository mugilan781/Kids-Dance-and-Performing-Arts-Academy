Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2025 • Effective: January 1, 2025

Contents

1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Your Data 3. Sharing of Data 4. Cookies 5. Data Security 6. Children's Privacy 7. Your Rights 8. Data Retention 9. Contact Us
Summary: We collect only what we need, protect it seriously, never sell it, and give you full control over your data. This policy covers our website, Parent Dashboard and all Artiste Academy services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account, enroll your child, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Parent/Guardian name, email address and phone number
  • Child's name, date of birth and gender
  • Health notes and emergency contact details provided for child safety purposes
  • Payment information (processed securely through our payment partner — we do not store card details)
  • Messages and inquiry content submitted through our contact form

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent and navigation path
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Referral source (how you found us)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal information to:

  • Process and manage your child's enrollment and class participation
  • Provide access to the Parent Dashboard
  • Send important communications about classes, recitals and fee payments
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Send newsletters and promotional content (only if you opt in)
  • Improve our website and services through analytics
  • Comply with legal obligations and safeguarding requirements
We will never use your child's information for marketing purposes or share it with third parties without your explicit consent.

3. Sharing of Data

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal data. We may share it only in these circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Payment processors, email service providers and cloud hosting services — bound by strict data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order or to protect the safety of children in our care
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, with appropriate notice to you

4. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website. These include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website and Parent Dashboard to function. Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your theme (light/dark) and language/direction preferences.
  • Analytics Cookies: Anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the site (Google Analytics). These require your consent.
  • Marketing Cookies: Only set if you have explicitly consented via the cookie banner.

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through our cookie banner or your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage of all personal data at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls ensuring only authorized staff can access personal data
  • Two-factor authentication on all administrative accounts

6. Children's Privacy

We take the privacy of children extremely seriously. Our services are directed to parents and guardians, not directly to children. When you enroll your child:

  • Your child's data is used solely for delivering our educational services
  • We do not engage in targeted advertising using children's data
  • Children's health and emergency information is accessible only to faculty on a need-to-know basis
  • We do not publish photos or videos of children without explicit written consent from their parent/guardian

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you and your child
  • Right of Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right of Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right of Restriction: Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right of Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing for marketing or profiling purposes

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@artisteacademy.in. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your child is enrolled with us and for up to 3 years thereafter, to comply with legal and safeguarding obligations. After this period, data is securely deleted or anonymized. You may request earlier deletion by contacting us.

9. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have any questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact our Data Protection Officer:

  • privacy@artisteacademy.in
  • Artiste Academy, 12 Arts Lane, Nungambakkam, Chennai 600034
  • +91 44 1234 5678

We are committed to resolving all privacy-related concerns promptly and transparently.
