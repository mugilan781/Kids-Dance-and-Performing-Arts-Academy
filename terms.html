Terms & Conditions

By enrolling your child with Kids Dance & Performing Arts Academy, you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions. Please read them carefully. If you have any questions, contact us at hello@kdpa.in.

1. Acceptance of Terms

These Terms & Conditions govern your use of KDPA's services including enrollment, class participation, Parent Dashboard access and attendance at recitals. By completing the enrollment process, you agree to all terms stated here on behalf of yourself and your child.

2. Enrollment Policy

Enrollment is confirmed upon receipt of the completed application and first term's fee payment. KDPA reserves the right to:

  • Place students in appropriate batches based on age and skill assessment
  • Limit batch sizes to a maximum of 12 students for quality instruction
  • Decline enrollment where batches are full (waitlist offered)
  • Reassign students to different batches as deemed appropriate by faculty

3. Fees & Payments

Fees are structured on a monthly or term basis as published on our website. All fees are due on or before the 5th day of each month or term start. Late payment incurs a ₹200 administrative fee. Fees are non-refundable after the 7th day of a term, except in documented cases of serious illness or family emergency (subject to management discretion).

  • Monthly fees must be paid by the 5th of each month
  • Term fees must be paid in full before the first class of the term
  • Bounced payments will incur a ₹500 bank charge recovery fee
  • Receipts will be issued via email within 48 hours of payment

4. Attendance Policy

Regular attendance is essential for skill development. We expect a minimum of 75% attendance per term. Students with attendance below 50% without valid reasons may be asked to repeat the current level in the next term.

  • Absences should be notified at least 2 hours before class via WhatsApp or phone
  • Makeup classes are offered for notified absences, subject to availability
  • Recurring unnotified absences may result in forfeiture of makeup privileges
  • Regular attendance is required for recital participation

5. Code of Conduct

KDPA is committed to providing a safe, respectful and nurturing environment. All students, parents and visitors are expected to:

  • Treat faculty, staff and fellow students with respect and kindness
  • Arrive on time and in appropriate attire as specified for each program
  • Refrain from disruptive behavior during classes and rehearsals
  • Respect academy property and studio equipment
  • Follow KDPA's zero-tolerance policy on bullying, harassment or discrimination

Serious or repeated violations of the code of conduct may result in suspension or termination of enrollment without fee refund.

6. Photography, Video & Social Media

KDPA may photograph or video record classes, rehearsals and recitals for promotional and archival purposes. By enrolling, you grant KDPA a non-exclusive license to use such content featuring your child for our website, social media and marketing materials. You may withdraw this consent at any time in writing.

Parents must obtain written permission from KDPA and the families of other children before publishing photos or videos that include other students.

7. Health & Safety

For the safety of all students:

  • Please keep your child home if they are unwell — do not bring a child with fever, vomiting or infectious illness to class
  • Inform us of any medical conditions, allergies or physical limitations at enrollment and update us promptly if these change
  • KDPA will administer first aid and contact parents/emergency contacts immediately in case of injury
  • KDPA is not responsible for pre-existing conditions that may be aggravated by normal dance or physical activity

8. Liability

Participation in dance, music and drama activities involves physical activity and carries an inherent risk of minor injury. While KDPA takes all reasonable precautions, we are not liable for injuries that occur as a result of activities unless caused by faculty negligence. Parents acknowledge this risk and confirm their child is physically able to participate. KDPA's liability is limited to the fees paid in the current term.

9. Cancellation & Withdrawal Policy

If you wish to withdraw your child from enrollment:

  • Provide written notice at least 30 days before the next term begins for full fee waiver
  • Withdrawals mid-term will not be eligible for a pro-rated refund after the 7th day of the term
  • KDPA reserves the right to terminate enrollment and issue a pro-rated refund if classes cannot be provided due to unforeseen circumstances (natural disasters, public health emergencies, etc.)

10. Changes to These Terms

KDPA reserves the right to update these Terms & Conditions. Significant changes will be communicated via email and through the Parent Dashboard at least 14 days before taking effect. Continued enrollment after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the new terms.

For questions about these terms, contact us at hello@kdpa.in or call +91 44 1234 5678.
