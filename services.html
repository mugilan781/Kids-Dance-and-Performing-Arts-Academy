Our Disciplines

Explore Every Art Form

Classical Dance

Immerse your child in the timeless beauty of Bharatanatyam and Kuchipudi — India's most revered classical dance forms. Our curriculum follows the traditional Kalakshetra method while incorporating modern pedagogical techniques for young learners.

Ages 4–16 Sat & Sun Batches Beginner to Advanced
Enroll in Classical Dance

Western Dance

From contemporary to jazz, lyrical to freestyle — our Western Dance program embraces the full spectrum of modern movement. Students develop physical awareness, rhythmic precision and expressive freedom.

Ages 5–17 Weekday Batches All Levels
Enroll in Western Dance

Hip-Hop & Street Dance

Groove to the rhythm of the streets! Our Hip-Hop program covers breaking, popping, locking, and freestyle. Kids develop musicality, coordination and most importantly — their own unique style.

Ages 6–17 Weekend Batches High Energy
Enroll in Hip-Hop

Classical Ballet

Rooted in centuries of tradition, our Ballet program follows the Royal Academy of Dance syllabus. From first positions to pointe work, we nurture grace, strength and artistic expression at every stage.

Ages 3–15 Daily Batches RAD Certified
Enroll in Ballet

Drama & Theater

Our Drama program uses method acting, improvisation and storytelling to build confidence, empathy and communication skills. Students stage two full productions per year at a professional theater.

Ages 6–16 Fri-Sun Batches 2 Productions/Year
Enroll in Drama

Music

Piano, violin, guitar, vocals and music theory — our Music department is led by conservatory-trained musicians. We develop musical literacy alongside technical skill, fostering a deep love for music from an early age.

Ages 4–17 Flexible Timings Individual & Group
Enroll in Music
